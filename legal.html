Informational demo interface. Last updated 2026

BTC Bridge is a non-custodial interface that helps you prepare and sign BTC and EVM transactions from your own wallets. It never holds your funds or keys. Nothing here is financial, investment or legal advice.

Privacy policy

This interface is a static front-end that works with public smart contracts. It has no accounts and does not request personal information.

  • No sign-up, email or password is collected by this interface.
  • Wallet connections happen in your browser; your private keys never leave your wallet.
  • Public blockchain data (addresses, balances, transactions) is read from the networks and is inherently public.
  • Third-party services you connect (wallets, RPC providers, routers, explorers) have their own privacy practices.

  • Wrapped BTC depends on a custodian, federation or signer network; if it fails or is compromised, the token may lose value or become unredeemable.
  • Smart contract and bridge bugs can cause loss of funds, and no audit, insurance or guarantee is implied.
  • Bitcoin and EVM transactions are irreversible; a wrong address or amount cannot be undone.
  • Network fees, gas and bridge fees vary and are paid by you; confirmation times depend on both networks.
  • Token names such as WBTC, tBTC and cbBTC are examples only; this interface is not official for or affiliated with any of them.
  • The interface is provided “as is”, without warranties of any kind, and may contain errors or become unavailable.

Verify the network, token and amount in your wallet before signing, and never share your seed phrase or private keys.
